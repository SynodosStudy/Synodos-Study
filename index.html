This study explores how individuals with distressful mental health experiences navigate everyday life and foster positive adaptation through meaningful social interactions. This study relies on a baseline self-reported survey and the Experience Sampling Methodology (ESM; a diary technique aimed at sampling people's behaviour several times a day through brief questionnaires sent to participants' smartphones through an application (m-path).

Who are eligible to participate?

We are inviting people 18 years old or above who are able to read and understand English to take part in our study. You may be eligible if you have ever experienced any of the following:

  • Feeling deeply sad, drained, and hopeless almost every day continuously for two weeks
  • Experiences of shifting between weeks of depression and separate periods of unusually high emotion or energy level for days at a time
  • Changes in vision/hearing/taste/sense of touch
  • Having unusual beliefs, struggling to keep thoughts straight, or feeling not quite like yourself

All prospective participants are asked to complete an online screening survey before formal invitation to the study.

What does this study involve?

The study consists:

  • an online baseline questionnaire completed at your own convenience at the beginning (about 20-25 minutes)
  • optional brief surveys regarding your daily experience for 2 weeks (i.e., experience sampling methodology [ESM] surveys) including:
    • a brief online preparation session via video conferencing (about 10 minutes)
    • a momentary experience survey collection for 2 weeks from your phone (about 7 minutes/day)

What do we offer in return?

    This study offers compensation to participants up to £16.60 (or the local equivalent) or 5 SONA credits:

    • £5 for successful completion of the baseline survey (or 1 SONA credit)
    • Up to £11.60 (bonus included) for ESM surveys (or up to 4 SONA credits)

Participants will need to be recruited through the SONA system and be a matriculated student at The University of Edinburgh to be offered SONA credits.
